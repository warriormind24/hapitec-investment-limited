About Us

Growing Zambia through quality piggery, construction, and supply

Located on Solwezi Road, Hapitec Investment Limited combines animal husbandry expertise with construction and material supply to serve businesses, communities, and households.

Hapitec Investment Limited is a dynamic company based in Copperbelt, Zambia, specializing in piggery, livestock consultancy, construction, and supply. We produce high-quality pigs for meat and live sales while offering construction and material solutions that support growth across residential, commercial, and institutional markets.

Under the leadership of CEO Mr. Happy Mulobeka and Director Ms. Prudence Kambafwile, we apply Total Quality Management and Kaizen practices to ensure consistent performance and customer satisfaction.

Our well-rounded and experienced team combines business, veterinary, and field expertise to deliver practical, reliable results for every client.
